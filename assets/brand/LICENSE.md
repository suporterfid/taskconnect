## Usage notes

- Do not stretch, recolor, rotate, outline, or add effects (shadows, glows,
  gradients) to the mark.
- Maintain clear space around the mark of at least the height of the
  rounded-square symbol on all sides.
- `mark.svg` (two-tone: `--color-purple-500` symbol, `--color-neutral-950`
  checkmark) and `wordmark.svg` are legible on both the dark canvas
  (`--color-canvas`, `#000000`, the primary case) and on white.
  `mark-monochrome.svg` uses `currentColor` with the checkmark cut out as
  negative space, for single-color contexts.
- Tested for legibility at 16px (favicon size) and at repository-avatar
  sizes.

See `docs/visual-identity-spec.md` §8 ("Project marks") and §12
("Repository asset guidance") for the broader design system these assets
sit inside.
